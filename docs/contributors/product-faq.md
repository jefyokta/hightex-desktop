# Product FAQ

## What is HighTex designed for?

HighTex is a thesis-specialist editor for strict, structured academic
documents. Its purpose is to help students follow academic formatting
requirements while focusing on their research, rather than manually formatting
a general-purpose document.

The document model includes thesis concepts such as chapters, citations,
references, figures, tables, equations, numbering, and generated sections.
Features should preserve these relationships and the consistency of the
compiled document.

## What is HighTex useful for?

HighTex helps students write theses while following academic formatting
requirements. It provides a structured thesis workflow so users can focus on
their research instead of manually formatting a general-purpose document.

## Is HighTex intended for arbitrary custom documents?

No. HighTex is not intended to be a general-purpose custom document editor.
Templates and variations are a good fit when they remain within the structured
thesis model. A feature that turns the document into arbitrary free-form
content may conflict with HighTex's purpose and should be evaluated as a
product-scope decision, not just an editor implementation task.
